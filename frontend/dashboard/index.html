🔍 Visual Findings
  • Yellow/orange to reddish-brown lesions visible on leaves
  • Rust-colored lesions consistent with plum rust
  • Multiple affected leaves indicate an established infection
🌿 Cultural Management
  • Remove heavily infected/fallen leaves where practical.
  • Maintain good canopy ventilation through appropriate pruning.
  • Avoid prolonged leaf wetness and excessive canopy humidity.
🧪 Chemical Management

Use a fungicide registered/labelled for plum rust in your region. Apply according to the product label, crop stage, and local agricultural recommendations.

🛡️ Preventive Protocol
  • Begin regular monitoring during periods favorable for rust.
  • Pay particular attention after prolonged rain, dew, or humid weather.
  • Inspect both upper and lower leaf surfaces.
