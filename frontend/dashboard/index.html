Factual Hallucination
Q: Kya gehu ki fasal august mein lagayi ja sakti hai?
❌ Base Model
"Yes, wheat can be grown in various seasons depending on the region and climate conditions."
✅ Fine-tuned
"Nahi, gehu Rabi fasal hai. Sahi time October-November hai. August mein lagane se fasal kharab hogi — temperature bahut zyada hoti hai."
Incomplete Answer
Q: PM-KISAN mein kitna paisa milta hai aur kaise register karein?
❌ Base Model
"PM-KISAN provides financial support to farmers under various government schemes for agricultural support."
✅ Fine-tuned
"PM-KISAN mein 6,000 rupaye/year milte hain — teen kiston mein (2,000 rupaye har 4 mahine). Register: pmkisan.gov.in ya CSC center. Documents: Aadhar + bank passbook + khasra."
Vague / Generic

❌ Base Model
"For rice blast disease, consult agricultural experts and use appropriate fungicides as recommended."
✅ Fine-tuned
"Chawal mein blast disease Magnaporthe oryzae fungus se hoti hai. Tricyclazole 75% WP 0.6g/litre spray karo. Kheton mein paani ka level sahi rakho. Agar zyada spread ho to Isoprothiolane use karo."
